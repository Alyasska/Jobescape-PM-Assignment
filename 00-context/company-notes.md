## Jobescape (the product under test)
- **Value prop:** teach professionals to apply AI (Claude) in their work → better results, faster.
- **Flagship course:** "Master the Claude." Vertical courses: Claude for Accountants / Lawyers / Sales / etc.
- **Acquisition:** Meta ads (US) → **quiz funnel** → **paywall** → product.
- **Scale:** ~1,000 new registrations/day.
- **Monetization:** subscription, 3 plans (1/4/12-week) + 2 one-time upsells at checkout. See `part-d-economics/data/plans.csv`.
- **Recent feature:** "Challenge" — 7-day challenge, one AI skill/day, to build a daily-return habit and lift first-week retention.

## Strategic read (why this matters for the answers)
- It's a **performance-marketing + subscription** business → the north star is **LTV vs CAC** and **early retention**. Every part of the assignment ladders up to that:
  - Part A/B = top of funnel (who we sell to, how we compare) → conversion + expectation-setting.
  - Part C = activation/retention (does Challenge build the habit that protects the subscription).
  - Part D = unit economics (what a user is actually worth; does a plan-upgrade test pay off).
- Meta-note the interviewer will love: Jobescape teaches people to use **Claude/AI** — so **doing this assignment heavily with AI agents is on-brand** and worth calling out in the submission.
