## Task 1 — Audience Segments → `01-segments.md`
1. **Ingest evidence:** review every ad creative (`materials/ad-creatives/`) and the quiz funnel
   answers (`materials/quiz-funnel-answers/`) + the live quiz. Note the recurring hooks in creatives
   (what pain/promise each sells) and the distribution of quiz answers (profession, age, goal, AI level,
   fear/blocker). The quiz answers are the strongest signal for **how to split**.
2. **Choose a split axis and justify it.** Candidates: by **job-to-be-done / goal** (get promoted, save
   time, don't get replaced by AI, earn more), by **profession vertical** (accountant/lawyer/sales/…),
   by **AI proficiency** (never used → power user), by **motivation** (fear-driven vs ambition-driven).
   Pick the axis that best predicts different product needs; you may use a primary + secondary axis.
3. For each segment give: **who they are · the job they're hiring us for · expectations & motivation on
   arrival · their pain/need.** 4–6 crisp segments beats 12 fuzzy ones. Table + a line on relative size.
4. State explicitly **what you split by and why** (the brief asks for this directly).

## Task 2 — Does the product meet their need → `02-product-gap.md`
Requires having gone through the paid product (screenshots in `materials/` or notes).
For the key segments, break down **expectation vs reality**:
- **Promised at entry** (from creatives + quiz) → **delivered inside** (product): line them up.
- **Pre-purchase doubts** (e.g. "will this actually apply to *my* job?", "is it just generic ChatGPT
  tips?", "will I stick with it?") → did the product resolve each, yes/no, with evidence.
- **Promise→gap:** what was pledged at the entry point but not delivered inside.
End with: **where is the MAIN gap, and what's the risk to NVS** (churn/refunds/bad CAC payback). Make it
a pointed, prioritized conclusion — this is the money question of Part A.
