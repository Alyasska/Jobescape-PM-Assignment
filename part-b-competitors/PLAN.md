# Part B — Competitors · agent brief

**Role:** Competitor Scout. **Blocked on:** nothing (web research). Can run in parallel with Part D.
Read `../BRIEF.md` Part B. Tools: WebSearch / WebFetch. Ground every claim in a source (link it).

## Frame first (do this before searching)
Jobescape's **job-to-be-done:** "help a working professional get measurably better/faster at their
job by using AI (Claude), sold via ads → quiz → subscription." Competitors = anyone solving that JTBD
for a similar audience, even with a different mechanic.

## Task 1 — Find competitors (direct vs indirect)
Search across these buckets, then classify:
- **Direct:** consumer "learn to use AI / ChatGPT for work" course products with quiz-funnel + subscription
  (e.g. AI upskilling apps, "ChatGPT for professionals" course brands, similar Meta-ad-driven edtech).
- **Indirect:** general AI-skills learning (Coursera/Udemy AI tracks, LinkedIn Learning), vertical AI
  tools that reduce the need to "learn," newsletters/communities (e.g. AI-for-work newsletters), and
  the incumbents themselves (ChatGPT/Claude free tutorials).
Deliver `01-competitors.md`: a table — name · what it is · direct/indirect · **why it's a competitor**
(which slice of our audience + which part of the JTBD it takes).

## Task 2 — How each serves its audience
For the ~4–7 most relevant, a teardown in `02-analysis.md`:
- audience served + how similar to ours (overlap on segment);
- which expectations/intents it addresses and **how exactly** — name the mechanics: quiz/funnel,
  onboarding, lesson format, gamification/streaks, community, pricing/paywall, guarantees;
- what's done **well** and **poorly** (be specific; screenshots or quotes if you can fetch them).
Go through their funnels where possible (many have public quiz funnels — walk them).

## Task 3 — What to take (prioritized)
`03-recommendations.md`: a prioritized list of concrete initiatives/mechanics to adopt.
- For each: what it is, which competitor it's borrowed from, **why it fits our audience**, and rough
  effort/impact. **Order them and justify the order** (impact on activation/retention/LTV first).
- Tie recommendations back to gaps found in Part A where possible (cross-reference).

## Quality bar
- Direct/indirect calls must be defensible on JTBD + audience overlap, not just "also AI."
- Recommendations must be specific mechanics ("add a 7-day streak with X"), not "improve onboarding."
