# Part B — Deep Teardown: The Premium & Media-Flywheel Incumbents

**Why these three (and not the structural twins):** Coursiv/Iro are Jobescape's *mirror* — same funnel, same price, same app. These three are the **ceiling and the benchmark**. **Section** sets the *positioning ceiling* (what a credible, premium "AI for knowledge workers" brand looks like and can charge). **The Rundown** sets the *owned-audience benchmark* (a compounding, low-CAC acquisition engine Jobescape completely lacks — today it is 100% rented paid ads). **Mimo** is the *UX best-practice exemplar* — the reference standard for the exact onboarding→early-paywall→gamified-retention loop Jobescape lives or dies on. Read together, they answer three questions Jobescape must answer: *what can we credibly charge for, how do we stop renting our audience, and how do we build the funnel/retention loop better than the twins.*

> Convention: **fact** = sourced claim with URL. **[inference]** = my read, not directly sourced. Prices marked "~" are approximate / funnel- or promo-dependent.

---

## 1) SECTION (sectionai.com) — the positioning ceiling & the "AI coach" pivot

### 1.1 Positioning & audience
- **Promise / identity:** build the new **"AI class" of knowledge workers** — "do your best work with AI." Founded **2019 by Scott Galloway** (NYU Stern professor); backed by General Catalyst, Learn Capital, GSV Ventures. ([businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230919273825/en/Section-Launches-Industry-Leading-AI-Curriculum-to-Build-the-New-%E2%80%9CAI-Class%E2%80%9D-of-Knowledge-Workers))
- **Audience:** knowledge workers — leaders, department heads, and individual contributors — plus a large **B2B/enterprise L&D** motion. Reach cited at **33,000+ students across 97 countries, 200+ company partners.** ([businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230919273825/en/Section-Launches-Industry-Leading-AI-Curriculum-to-Build-the-New-%E2%80%9CAI-Class%E2%80%9D-of-Knowledge-Workers))
- **Credibility signal = the whole moat.** The Galloway brand is the acquisition engine and the trust premium. Section can put NYU-professor gravitas, named guest experts (e.g., a Q&A with AI figure **Yoshua Bengio** cited on the pricing page), and "Mini-MBA" framing behind the product — credentials Jobescape structurally cannot fake. ([sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing))
- **Closeness to Jobescape:** **audience ~identical** (broad non-technical professionals who want to "use AI at work"), **delivery opposite** (premium live/cohort + an AI-coach SaaS, not a $40/4-week habit app). Section is the *aspirational* end of the same demand curve — the brand Jobescape's buyer graduates *to*, or is told they can't afford. **[inference]**

### 1.2 Acquisition engine
- **Owned audience, not the ad auction.** Galloway's **No Mercy / No Malice** newsletter reaches **400,000–500,000+ subscribers**, and subscribing explicitly opts you into "emails from Scott Galloway and his edtech startup, Section." His broader Prof G Media portfolio (podcasts, YouTube, Substack) does **~$20M/yr**. ([on.substack](https://on.substack.com/p/scott-galloway-had-the-audience-heres), [profgmedia](https://www.profgmedia.com/s/no-mercy-no-malice))
- **Mechanic:** free media / huge personal brand → email list → workshop & cohort enrollments → membership + enterprise contracts. Paid ads exist but are secondary; **the founder's audience is the funnel.** This is the same media-flywheel logic as The Rundown, one tier more premium. **[inference]**

### 1.3 Product, format & mechanics
- **Two products fused:** (a) **live cohort courses + on-demand library** (~50 AI courses spanning "Generative AI Business Strategy," "AI for Personal Productivity," "AI Prompt Writing," plus role/function tracks) and an **"AI for Business Mini-MBA"**; delivery is **live, cohort-based, fast**, with office hours and certificates. ([businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230919273825/en/Section-Launches-Industry-Leading-AI-Curriculum-to-Build-the-New-%E2%80%9CAI-Class%E2%80%9D-of-Knowledge-Workers))
- **(b) The pivot that matters: "SectionAI," an AI coaching product.** Section is repackaging itself from *courses* to an **AI skills coach / "strategic co-pilot"** — you input company context, pick a framework (SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces), and it generates a structured strategy report from Section's proprietary frameworks + case studies. Pricing tiers are now organized around **"AI coaching"** and **"saveable AI use cases,"** not just course seats (see 1.4). ([skywork review](https://skywork.ai/skypage/en/sectionai-review-scott-galloway-ai-tool/1977558622344253440), [sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing))
- **Strategic read:** Section saw the same thing Jobescape must — *courses alone don't retain.* They are grafting a **recurring, usage-based AI tool** onto the education brand so there's a reason to keep paying after the course ends. This is the single most important competitive signal in this whole teardown. **[inference]**

### 1.4 Pricing & monetization
- **Current individual tiers** ([sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing)):
  - **Basic — Free:** basic AI coaching + **5 saveable AI use cases.**
  - **Premium — ~$62.50/mo billed annually (~$750/yr):** unlimited AI coaching, unlimited use cases, use-case library, unrestricted live + on-demand courses, AI certificate programs, monthly expert office hours.
- **Legacy tiers still visible:** AI Academy **On-Demand ~$41/mo** and **Unlimited ~$82/mo** (annual billing; the $82 tier bundles **40% off Mini-MBAs**). Mini-MBAs are priced higher (multi-hundred to four-figure). ([sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing), [help.sectionai](https://help.sectionai.com/support/solutions/articles/67000737537-what-membership-plans-do-you-offer-))
- **B2B is the real business:** small-team and >1,000-employee enterprise plans (custom pricing, SSO, LMS integration, admin dashboards, a "Head of AI training," benchmarking). ([sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing))
- **Takeaway on price band:** Section's individual price (~$750/yr) sits **~1.5–2.5× Jobescape's ~$300–520/yr band** — it proves there's willingness-to-pay *above* Jobescape when the brand and the "coach" justify it, and that **B2B/L&D is where the margin actually lives.**

### 1.5 What they do WELL / POORLY
**Well:**
- **Zero-CAC credibility.** A founder-brand that pre-sells trust; no other player here can match the "NYU professor / Mini-MBA" halo. ([on.substack](https://on.substack.com/p/scott-galloway-had-the-audience-heres))
- **Freemium built around an AI tool, not free lessons.** The free tier gives *basic coaching + 5 use cases* — a genuinely useful sample that also seeds switching-cost (your saved use cases). ([sectionai pricing](https://www.sectionai.com/pricing))
- **Dual monetization:** consumer membership *and* a defensible enterprise L&D pipeline (200+ partners). ([businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230919273825/en/Section-Launches-Industry-Leading-AI-Curriculum-to-Build-the-New-%E2%80%9CAI-Class%E2%80%9D-of-Knowledge-Workers))

**Poorly:**
- **The AI coach is narrow & needs babysitting.** Reviews say it's *"not designed for creative or high-volume content writing,"* output *"demands significant human refinement,"* and **max value requires already knowing Section's frameworks** — i.e., it's most useful to people who least need it. ([skywork review](https://skywork.ai/skypage/en/sectionai-review-scott-galloway-ai-tool/1977558622344253440))
- **Priced above generalist tools with no free trial on the AI product** — a hard sell vs. ChatGPT for a casual buyer. ([skywork review](https://skywork.ai/skypage/en/sectionai-review-scott-galloway-ai-tool/1977558622344253440))
- **Live/cohort delivery doesn't scale to Jobescape's volume economics** and gates the audience by time-zone/schedule — the opposite of a habit app. **[inference]**
- **Founder-dependency risk:** the brand *is* one person. **[inference]**

### 1.6 Steal-list for Jobescape
1. **Graft a recurring AI "coach/tool" onto the courses so retention isn't hostage to lesson supply.** Section is racing from "courses" to "a co-pilot you keep paying for." Jobescape already ships an AI chatbot — reframe it from "practice buddy" to a **role-specific work co-pilot** (e.g., "Accountant AI," "Sales AI") with **saveable, reusable use-cases/templates.** WHY it fits: content is commoditized/free (the brief's core threat) — the *durable* value and the churn defense is an always-there tool tied to the user's actual job, and saved use-cases create switching cost the way Section's do.
2. **Add a premium, higher-trust tier + a LinkedIn-worthy credential to capture willingness-to-pay above the $40/4-week band.** Section proves buyers will pay ~$750/yr for the *same audience* when a credibility wrapper (Mini-MBA framing, expert office hours, certificate) is present. Jobescape already issues LinkedIn certificates — productize a **"Pro/Certified" tier** at a higher price. WHY: expands ARPU and hedges the race-to-the-bottom against cheaper twins (Iro at $60/yr).
3. **Open a B2B / team-seat (L&D) motion.** Section's durable margin is enterprise, not $750 memberships. Jobescape's role-based courses (Accountants/Lawyers/Sales/HR/PM) map cleanly to team training. WHY: B2B has far higher LTV, near-zero incremental content cost, and diversifies away from paid-ads-only consumer CAC — directly relevant to Part D economics.

## 2) THE RUNDOWN / RUNDOWN UNIVERSITY (rundown.ai) — the media flywheel Jobescape doesn't have

### 2.1 Positioning & audience
- **Two-layer identity:** the free **daily newsletter** ("stay ahead in AI in ~5–10 min/day," distilling launches for non-technical pros) and the paid **Rundown University** ("practical AI courses & guides"). Founder **Rowan Cheung** (Forbes-profiled). ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university), [forbes](https://www.forbes.com/profile/rowan-cheung/))
- **Audience:** non-technical professionals who want to *use* AI — newsletter reaches **2,000,000+ readers** "from companies like Apple, OpenAI, NASA," + ~1.5M social followers. **Same buyer as Jobescape**, reached through owned media instead of a quiz ad. ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university), [eomag](https://www.eomag.io/article/the-rundown-ai-rowan-cheung))
- **Closeness:** high on audience + "learn to use AI" promise; different shape (media→course/community, not ads→app).

### 2.2 Acquisition engine — the whole point of this teardown
- **The newsletter IS the CAC-suppressor.** Bootstrapped; **$3M revenue in 2024, projected $7M in 2025** from subscriptions + AI workshops + sponsors (Google, Salesforce, Amazon). The free list is the compounding asset that feeds the paid product. ([eomag](https://www.eomag.io/article/the-rundown-ai-rowan-cheung))
- **How the list itself was built (the replicable part):**
  - **beehiiv Boosts / Ad Network — paid but cheap & compounding:** added **10,000+ subscribers at ~$2 each** via Boosts; the beehiiv Ad Network became "one of their most significant channels," and Cheung planned to **shift ~50% of marketing budget from Meta to beehiiv ads** (newsletter-to-newsletter acquisition converts warmer than cold Meta traffic). ([beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/why-the-rundown-founder-says-the-beehiiv-ad-network-is-the-best-newsletter-growth-channel), [blog.beehiiv 100k](https://blog.beehiiv.com/p/how-these-100k-subscriber-ai-newsletters-leverage-beehiiv))
  - **Referral program** (subscribers share for rewards) + a heavy X/Twitter presence (**0→300k in ~4 months, split ~150k email / 150k X**). ([creatorspotlight](https://www.creatorspotlight.com/p/the-rundown), [blog.beehiiv 100k](https://blog.beehiiv.com/p/how-these-100k-subscriber-ai-newsletters-leverage-beehiiv))
- **Read:** every free issue is both retention *and* a monetizable ad slot *and* a warm top-of-funnel for the $1k/yr course — a **rented-CAC hedge Jobescape has zero of today.** **[inference]**

### 2.3 Product, format & mechanics
- **Rundown University = hybrid live + on-demand + community + tool-bundle** ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university)):
  - **25+ expert-led courses**, self-selected by role/goal (e.g., *Intro to Vibe Coding, AI Growth Systems, Claude Cowork, Become an AI-Native Leader, AI Presentation Tools, "Finally Getting AI to Do Real Work"*).
  - **Weekly live workshops** with practitioners from Meta, Windsurf, Lindy AI etc. (Q&A + live demos), then **archived into the on-demand library.**
  - **100+ short (<15 min) daily video guides** — a *content-velocity* engine that keeps the paid product feeling fresh daily (mirrors the newsletter's daily cadence).
  - **Certificates** ("practical AI ability to employers") and a **"coming soon" community** to browse/remix others' workflows.
- **Social proof:** **1,179 members, 4.5★.** ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))

### 2.4 Pricing & monetization
- **Rundown Starter — Free:** live courses + 100+ daily guides (a generous free tier that doubles as funnel). ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))
- **Rundown Pro — ~$84/mo billed annually (~$1,008/yr), 7-day trial:** all 25+ courses, weekly expert calls, **community**, plus **"$1,600+ in annual subscriptions to top AI tools"** (Manus, Granola, Synthesia, Higgsfield, Typeless) bundled in. ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))
- **Killer monetization detail:** the page states **~70% of members expense it through their employer's L&D budget** — they deliberately price/position at the *"expense-it"* threshold, not the *"personal discretionary spend"* threshold. That's a different, higher LTV buyer psychology than Jobescape's consumer-card default. ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))
- **Diversified revenue:** newsletter **sponsorships** (max 2–3 labeled slots/issue) + University subscriptions + workshops. ([eomag](https://www.eomag.io/article/the-rundown-ai-rowan-cheung), [readless](https://www.readless.app/blog/is-the-rundown-ai-newsletter-free-2026))

### 2.5 What they do WELL / POORLY
**Well:**
- **Compounding, low-CAC owned audience** that simultaneously retains, monetizes (ads), and feeds the paid product — the durable answer to rising Meta CAC. ([beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/why-the-rundown-founder-says-the-beehiiv-ad-network-is-the-best-newsletter-growth-channel))
- **"Expense-it" positioning** → higher price point, softer price sensitivity. ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))
- **Bundled tool subscriptions** make a $1,008 price feel *net-positive* ("$1,600 of tools included") — a brilliant value-framing trick. ([rundown.ai/ai-university](https://www.rundown.ai/ai-university))
- **Daily content velocity** keeps both free and paid products alive without a course feeling "finished."

**Poorly:**
- **Content is the product = commoditization risk** (same threat named in the Jobescape brief) — a free newsletter is trivially substitutable (Superhuman AI, TLDR, Mindstream). ([readless review](https://www.readless.app/blog/the-rundown-ai-newsletter-review-2026)) **[inference]**
- **$1,008/yr is steep for a self-directed, "customize your own path" product** — high-agency required; likely churns non-self-starters. **[inference]**
- **Newsletter growth partly *bought*** (Boosts/Ad Network) — cheaper than Meta but still not free, and beehiiv-dependent. ([beehiiv blog](https://www.beehiiv.com/blog/why-the-rundown-founder-says-the-beehiiv-ad-network-is-the-best-newsletter-growth-channel)) **[inference]**

### 2.6 Steal-list for Jobescape
1. **Launch an owned free newsletter (or in-app daily brief) as a rented-CAC hedge.** The Rundown's entire economic advantage over Jobescape is that its audience *compounds and costs ~$2 once*, vs. Jobescape paying Meta again for every install. Start a role-segmented "5-min daily AI-at-work" brief, grow it via **beehiiv Boosts / Ad Network + a referral program** (converts warmer than cold Meta, and the brief becomes both a retention surface and a re-engagement channel for churned app users). WHY it fits: directly attacks Jobescape's single biggest fragility — 100% paid-ads dependence — and Part D break-even math improves the moment blended CAC drops.
2. **Bundle real third-party AI tool access so the price reads as net-positive.** "$1,600 of tools included" reframes a subscription as a *saving*. Jobescape could negotiate credits/discounts to real tools its ICP already wants. WHY: neutralizes the "why pay when the content is free?" objection the brief flags — the buyer pays for *access + curation*, not lessons.
3. **Re-anchor a premium tier to the "expense-it / employer L&D" threshold.** ~70% of Rundown Pro is employer-expensed; they priced *for that wallet.* Jobescape can add "get reimbursed" copy, an invoice/receipt feature, and role-based framing to move part of the base onto the corporate wallet. WHY: employer-funded LTV dwarfs consumer-card LTV and slashes price sensitivity — a cheap lever with outsized effect on Part D unit economics.

## 3) MIMO (getmimo.com) — the onboarding & early-paywall best-practice exemplar

*(Topic is coding, not "AI for your job" — so this is a **mechanics/UX** teardown, not a head-to-head. Mimo is the reference standard for the exact funnel loop Jobescape runs.)*

### 3.1 Positioning & audience
- "Learn coding/AI on the go" — **mobile-first, gamified, 2-minute lessons** for beginners who learn in idle moments. ([mimo.org](https://mimo.org/), [betalist](https://betalist.com/startups/mimo))
- Relevance to Jobescape: **identical monetization pattern** (paid ads → app onboarding → early paywall → freemium subscription → streak/XP retention). Mimo has spent years A/B-optimizing this exact loop, so it's the cleanest place to steal tactics.

### 3.2 Acquisition & scale
- Paid ads → app-store funnel; **~70,000–80,000 downloads/mo and ~$200k/mo revenue** (Sensor Tower–based estimate). IAPs span **$9.99–$99.99** (monthly ~$9.99–$12.49; annual ~$29.99–$99.99). ([adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/mimo/), plus estimate cited in [sensortower overview](https://app.sensortower.com/overview/1133960732?country=US))
- Growth is run as a **structured full-funnel experimentation practice** (Head of Growth Ekaterina Gamsriegler) — ASO + onboarding + paywall all continuously tested. ([purchasely](https://www.purchasely.com/blog/mimo-experimentation-ekaterina-gamsriegler))

### 3.3 Product, format & mechanics — the onboarding→paywall→retention loop (the gold)
Sequence (screensdesign teardown timestamps + App Fuel/coddy) ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mimo-learn-codingprogramming), [theappfuel](https://www.theappfuel.com/examples/mimo_onboarding), [coddy](https://coddy.tech/vs/mimo)):
- **Long, gamified onboarding — cited as ~15 core steps within a ~33-screen flow;** "one of the most gamified onboarding flows on the stores," engineered with "several dopamine shots to push you to come back." (Length is deliberate: each question increases *sunk-cost/commitment* before the ask.)
- **Personalization by feel, not forms:** a **playful experience *slider*** to self-rate skill "rather than boring radio buttons," plus early affirmation copy ("**You'll fit right in**," ~0:21) to lower anxiety and build belonging *before* asking for money.
- **Paywall appears EARLY (~1:21 in the flow), before deep value delivery**, but de-risked with a **14-day free trial** and stacked with **social proof (testimonials, "App of the Day" badge)**. Post-subscribe, an **animated trial countdown** makes the transaction feel rewarding rather than extractive.
- **First lesson is hands-on fast (~2:38):** complete a real code snippet, see the result in a live browser preview (~3:45) — an **immediate competence/"aha" hit** that justifies the trial you just started.
- **Retention loop:** **streaks, XP, leaderboard** (dashboard ~7:46) + a **Store** selling *unlimited hearts / streak repairs* (~5:42) — i.e., the gamification isn't just engagement, it's a **second monetization surface** (Duolingo-style loss-aversion economy).

### 3.4 Pricing & monetization
- **Freemium**: free tier deliberately capped (a few lessons/day, then a paywall prompt); **Pro ~$5.99/mo or ~$49.99/yr, 14-day trial**, most content Pro-gated. ([coddy](https://coddy.tech/vs/mimo), [adapty](https://adapty.io/paywall-library/mimo/))
- **Proven paywall experiment (the one to copy exactly):** the **"Blinkist paywall"** — a **free-trial *timeline* screen + a reminder notification before the trial ends** — **doubled trial opt-in (+100%) and cut first-24h cancellations by 25%.** ([purchasely](https://www.purchasely.com/blog/mimo-experimentation-ekaterina-gamsriegler)) This is a concrete, high-ROI, directly-portable tactic.

### 3.5 What they do WELL / POORLY
**Well:**
- **Commitment-escalating onboarding** — long, playful, personalized; every step raises sunk cost so the paywall lands on a warmed-up user. ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mimo-learn-codingprogramming))
- **Trial *transparency* as a conversion lever, not a leak** — the trial-timeline + pre-expiry reminder *increased* opt-ins AND *reduced* early cancels simultaneously (rare win-win). ([purchasely](https://www.purchasely.com/blog/mimo-experimentation-ekaterina-gamsriegler))
- **Fast competence hit** right after the paywall justifies the spend. ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mimo-learn-codingprogramming))
- **Gamification doubles as monetization** (hearts/streak-repair store). ([screensdesign](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/mimo-learn-codingprogramming))

**Poorly / risks (also a warning for Jobescape's own paywall):**
- **Paywall-before-value + auto-renew trials draw refund/"felt tricked" complaints** — a known pattern for this funnel archetype (the same disclosure risk flagged for Coursiv). ([coddy](https://coddy.tech/vs/mimo)) **[inference]**
- **Depth ceiling:** great for habit + beginners, thin for real mastery — retention decays once novelty/streak pressure fades. ([coddy](https://coddy.tech/vs/mimo)) **[inference]**

### 3.6 Steal-list for Jobescape
1. **Copy the "Blinkist paywall" mechanic verbatim: a trial-*timeline* screen + a pre-expiry reminder notification.** It's a proven +100% trial opt-in / -25% early-cancel at Mimo — nearly free to build, and it makes the trial feel *fair* (which cuts exactly the refund/chargeback problem that plagues this funnel type). WHY: Jobescape's economics live and die on trial→paid conversion and refund rate (Part D); this is the single highest-ROI, lowest-effort lever available.
2. **Escalate commitment with a longer, feel-based, personalized onboarding *before* the paywall — and land a hands-on "aha" immediately after.** Replace form-y quiz steps with sliders + affirmation copy ("you'll fit right in"), then, right after payment, deliver a genuine *"I just did real work with AI"* win (e.g., generate a usable email/spreadsheet formula in the user's own role). WHY: Jobescape already runs a quiz — Mimo shows the quiz should be a *commitment device*, and that post-paywall competence is what converts a trial into a retained subscriber for a commoditized-content product.
3. **Turn the streak/XP economy into a *second* monetization + loss-aversion retention surface.** Mimo sells hearts/streak-repairs; Jobescape's "Duolingo for AI" loop can add streak-freeze, cosmetic ranks, or role-leaderboards that both deepen the habit and add incremental revenue. WHY: it defends retention (the weakest point when the underlying content is free) and lifts ARPU without new content cost — again a direct Part D lever.
