# Part B — Direct Competitors of Jobescape (Research, Step 1)

**Scope:** consumer products that teach *non-technical* professionals/knowledge workers how to **use AI to work better** — sold as courses, apps, cohorts, workshops, newsletters-with-courses, or subscriptions — aimed mostly at Tier-1 (US/EU) users.

**Jobescape reference model:** Meta ads → quiz funnel → paywall → app subscription ("Duolingo for AI" with an AI chatbot). Flagship "Master the Claude" + role courses (Accountants / Lawyers / Sales / HR / Marketing / PM). Free-to-start, LinkedIn certificates. Markets: US & Western Europe. (Jobescape home + course page: https://jobescape.me/ , https://jobescape.me/courses/master-the-claude ; App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jobescape-ai-courses-tools/id6752938888)

**How "closeness" is judged:** a competitor is *closest* to Jobescape when it matches on **all four** axes — (a) audience = non-technical pros, (b) format = habit-loop app or short-course, (c) monetization = consumer subscription, (d) acquisition = paid-ads + quiz/tripwire funnel. The nearer to 4/4, the more direct.

> Convention: **fact** = sourced claim with URL. **[inference]** = my read. Prices marked "~" are approximate or funnel-dependent (many of these use dynamic quiz-based pricing, so a single sticker price rarely exists).

## Per-competitor notes

### Cluster 1 — Quiz-funnel / app-subscription (the structural twins — most important)

**1. Coursiv (coursiv.io) — DIRECT, closest structural twin.**
Positioning: "AI courses for beginners — learn AI skills step by step"; an "AI gym" where you *use* AI to get real results, not just watch lessons. Audience: non-technical beginners/busy pros, US/EU. Format: web-to-app subscription; short daily lessons (audio, step-by-step guides, interactive), signature **28-Day AI Challenge**. Mechanics/onboarding: short **personality-style quiz** tailors content and sets price expectation; learning paths by outcome (marketing/sales/PM). Pricing: dynamic — small **tripwire (~$6–20)** rolls into **~$39.99 every 4 weeks (~$300–520/yr)**; billing lives on Coursiv's side (web), not app-store. Acquisition: paid social → quiz funnel → web paywall. Notable: **hit ~$1.8M revenue in year one** on funnel design; also heavy Reddit/Play-Store complaints about the price-jump disclosure. Developer entity: **Zimran** (Android pkg `io.zimran.coursiv`). *Why direct:* 4/4 axis match — this is the Jobescape playbook, aimed at the same buyer. Mechanics: **Prompt Lab** (feedback on real prompts), live "duels," XP/streaks/ranks, daily challenges; 2026 additions include AI-generated Custom Paths + built-in AI coach. Pricing: free to start; **Pro $9.99/mo or $59.99/yr** (7-day trial). ## Deep-teardown shortlist

The 5–6 worth a full teardown for Part B — chosen for **closeness to Jobescape's model** and **strategic lessons** (funnel, positioning, monetization):

1. **Coursiv** — the closest **structural twin**: paid-ads → quiz funnel → web-to-app subscription → 28-day challenge, at Jobescape's exact price band, with proven revenue (~$1.8M yr-1). Teardown reveals the direct benchmark for funnel, paywall disclosure, retention, and churn/refund risk.
2. **Iro AI** — the **product/positioning clone**: explicitly "Duolingo for AI" (Jobescape's own tagline), same non-technical ICP, Prompt Lab + gamified practice. Best head-to-head to define Jobescape's product differentiation and pricing (Iro is far cheaper at $59.99/yr).
3. **Section** — the **credible premium incumbent** for the same knowledge-worker audience (Scott Galloway brand, 33k+ students, role-based AI curriculum + Mini-MBAs). Teardown = positioning ceiling, pricing power, and the "trust/credential" angle Jobescape must answer.
4. **Outskill** — the **high-ticket funnel benchmark**: free/cheap 2-day workshop → $1–3k cohort via heavy paid ads. Shows an alternate monetization (tripwire → high-ticket) and the paid-acquisition economics Jobescape competes against in the same ad auctions.
5. **The Rundown / Rundown University** — the **media-flywheel** model: a free newsletter as a compounding, low-CAC acquisition engine feeding a ~$1,008/yr course-and-community product. Teardown = the owned-audience acquisition channel Jobescape lacks (all paid-ads today).
6. **Be10x** — the **tripwire-funnel CRO exemplar**: a ₹9 3-hour workshop → mastery upsell, massive scale. A masterclass in front-of-funnel conversion and social proof — with the explicit caveat that its India-centric audience differs from Jobescape's US/EU target (useful as a contrast on geo/pricing).
